## Interpretation

- `Covered` is intentionally not used as a blanket label: the materials are introductory and do not provide an independently runnable end-to-end production workflow. [COURSE: 00 Expert Guide C2 Kickoff.pdf#slide 4] [GAP]
- Case-study claims about production usage and estimated business value are course evidence about those cases only; they are not implementation instructions or independently validated outcomes.
