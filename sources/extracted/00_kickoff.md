# 00 - Program Kickoff

## Summary

- The program is a six-week, cohort-based introduction to AI and machine learning, combining selected self-paced materials, weekly expert sessions, and peer discussion. [COURSE: 00 Expert Guide C2 Kickoff.pdf#slides 3-5]
- The stated goal is foundational literacy that helps learners identify opportunities; the kickoff does not teach a production ML lifecycle. [COURSE: 00 Expert Guide C2 Kickoff.pdf#slide 4] [GAP]

## Key Concepts

- Learning is organized around planning AI implementation, ML essentials, supervised and unsupervised learning, neural networks, convolutional/recurrent networks, applied ML, and introductory GenAI. [COURSE: 00 Expert Guide C2 Kickoff.pdf#slides 4-5]
- The program expects self-paced study, participation in live events and discussions, and periodic feedback. [COURSE: 00 Expert Guide C2 Kickoff.pdf#slides 6-7]

## Worked Examples and Case Studies

- The kickoff gives work examples in supply-chain forecasting, computer vision, anomaly detection, robotics, and agentic automation, but these are introductions rather than implementation walkthroughs. [COURSE: 00_Expert_Guide_Kickoff_Transcript.txt#lines 115-164] [GAP]
